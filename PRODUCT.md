# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + plain HTML/CSS/JS (confirmed by user). GSAP + ScrollTrigger, Lenis, Outfit self-hosted via @fontsource-variable. Images pre-processed to AVIF/WebP/JPG with sharp (`scripts/images.mjs`).

## Users

Women in and around Moerbeke (Oost-Vlaanderen) looking for a hairdresser for colour, cuts, brushing or hair extensions, who want a personal, calm appointment rather than a high-street salon. They arrive mostly on mobile, often via Instagram or Facebook, and the job is: understand who Tim is, feel the place, and book.

## Product Purpose

One-page site for Salon Dola, a ladies' hair salon run by Tim from his home. Success: visitors call or leave an appointment request; the site feels like the private atelier of a fashion house.

## Positioning

Kleur & haarextensionspecialist. Dameskapsalon. Op afspraak. Salon at Tim's home, one client at a time. Tim works with his own extension brand and his own method, and does not treat extensions placed elsewhere.

## Brand Commitments

- Pure typographic logo: "SALON" thin with very wide tracking, "DOLA." large. Files in `salon-dola-logo/`.
- Black and white only (--ink #141414, --ink-2 #1E1E1E, --paper #FAFAFA, --mute #8A8A8A). No accent colour.
- Outfit only. One family.
- Copy in Dutch, first person (Tim), visitor addressed as "je". Confident, sober, short. No jokes, exclamation marks, em dashes, emoji or decorative icons.
- No animals as a theme or section; they may appear incidentally in photos.
- The point after "DOLA." is the signature element.

1. Personal over commercial: Tim speaks, one client at a time.
2. Calm is the luxury: slow motion, no noise.
3. Typography is the brand.
4. Booking is always one step away.
